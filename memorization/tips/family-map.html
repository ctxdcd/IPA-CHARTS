Vowel family map

Short vowels as a family tree

To make it easier to remember think that as A, I, U, E, O are the “head of the family.” In English, each one has different “children” based on duration, tongue position, and mouth shape.

Family A Short

A

Anak 1 · Si Lebar /æ/ — short, very open mouth. Example: cat
Anak 2 · Si Tajam /ʌ/ — short, central sound. Example: cup
Family I Short

I

Anak 1 · Si Cepat /ɪ/ — short, relaxed. Example: sit
Family U Short

U

Anak 1 · Si Pendek /ʊ/ — quick and short. Example: foot
Family E Short

E

Anak 1 · Si Pemalas /ə/ — very weak and unstressed. Example: teacher
Family O Short

O

Anak 1 · Si Kaget /ɒ/ — short, a little open. Example: hot
