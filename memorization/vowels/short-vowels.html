What is a short vowel?

Monophthongs (pure vowels) are constant sounds produced in a fixed position without gliding. They are divided by duration: Short vowels are brief and relaxed (e.g., /ɪ, e, æ, ʌ, ɒ, ʊ, ə/), while Long vowels are sustained for a longer period (e.g., /iː, uː, ɔː, ɜː, ɑː/).

Think of it as a quick vowel sound, not a stretched one.
